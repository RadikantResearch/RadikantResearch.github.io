Products

A selection of products where I served as Lead Design Engineer and Project Manager. I managed the 3D design, PCBAs, BOMs, firmware, mass manufacturing, and those pesky certifications (FCC, RED, IC, CE, Prop 65). Developed alongside industry experts, several of these became top sellers in their categories.

PowerBlow

Interactive Suction Controller

PowerBlow is a low-entry product within the Kiiroo ecosystem that I conceived and led from idea to mass production. I was responsible for the product’s design, engineering, project management, compliance, and manufacturing. The device went on to become one of Kiiroo’s best-selling products.

Control

Interactive Motion Controller

The Kiiroo Control device is designed as an ultra low-cost, compact motion controller for seamless compatibility with the Kiiroo Stroker range.
